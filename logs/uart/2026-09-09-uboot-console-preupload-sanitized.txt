GAPD-7500 U-Boot console and pre-upload checkpoint
Date: 2026-09-09 (Asia/Seoul)
Target: owner-controlled actual router, stock 1.06.08

Verified transition
- The stock `dwlee` reference uses `bootdelay_=1` as its U-Boot access marker.
  The destructive `dwlee` script itself was not executed.
- The marker was absent before the test, written with the stock environment
  tool, and read back as `bootdelay_=1`.
- A procd software reboot was used; no power cycle occurred.
- One SPACE byte was transmitted only after the exact console-ready UART
  signature. The actual `IPQ6018#` prompt was observed.
- No other pre-prompt byte was transmitted.

Rollback at U-Boot
- `bootdelay_=1` was confirmed in U-Boot RAM, removed, and confirmed absent.
- One `saveenv` completed with erase and write success, persisting the marker
  removal before any temporary network value was set.
- `ipaddr=192.168.219.1`, `serverip=192.168.219.100`, and
  `netmask=255.255.255.0` were set and read back in RAM only.
- No later `saveenv` was issued.

Current pre-upload blocker
- U-Boot `eth0` is present on IPQ MDIO0.
- PHY0 through PHY4 reported Down during two ping attempts.
- Windows reported Ethernet 12 Disconnected with zero link speed.
- A different physical jack must be tested before TFTP.

Safety boundary
- The router is intentionally idle at the U-Boot prompt.
- No `tftpboot`, OpenWrt image transfer, hash command, or `bootm` was issued.
- OpenWrt transferred bytes: 0.
- No firmware, MTD/UBI volume, calibration data, or boot selection was written.
